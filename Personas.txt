
UNIVERSITY CLASSROOM LOCATOR
USER ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

1. ADMIN
    - Manage classroom records
    - Add and update building information
    - Add and update classroom information
    - Add and update classroom schedules
    - Manage room availability information


2. FACULTY / TEACHER
    - View classroom schedules
    - Check classroom availability
    - Locate classrooms within the campus
    - View building and floor information
    - Check assigned classroom information


3. STUDENT
    - Search for classrooms
    - Locate classrooms within the campus
    - Check classroom availability
    - View classroom schedules
    - View building and floor information
